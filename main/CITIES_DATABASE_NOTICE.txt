City database source

The offline city database is generated from the GeoNames cities15000 export:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip

Country names are taken from:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/countryInfo.txt

GeoNames data is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
License. The database contains cities with population 15,000 or more and
uses the IANA timezone identifier supplied by GeoNames.
